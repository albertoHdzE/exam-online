   On proposal 2 — real-time console input. Yes, with one physical constraint to design around: when   
   you're in the exam window, your keystrokes go to the browser, not our terminal — global hotkeys work

   Two risks to manage, both solvable:                                                                 
                                                                                                       
   • Precedence conflicts: free-form text can contradict the OCR'd problem. Rule: user directives      
     override style/approach, never the stated problem behavior.

     background directive reader must share one input arbiter, or we'll get lost keystrokes. Simplest  
     safe design: directives are only read at well-defined points (F9 entry, or "type before pressing  
     F7"), not a free-running thread.
